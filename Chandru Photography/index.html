About Us

Where Every Frame Tells a Story

At Chandru Capture Photography, we believe photography is more than simply taking a picture — it is the art of preserving emotions, stories, and unforgettable moments for a lifetime.

Led by a passionate professional photographer with over 14 years of experience, Chandru Capture brings together refined visual artistry, creative direction, modern camera technology, and a distinctive aesthetic to every project.

Based in Ramanathapuram, we specialize in creating elegant and emotionally rich imagery across Wedding, Event, Candid, Portrait, Baby Shoot, and Model Shoot Photography. Our approach is centered around authentic connections, natural expressions, beautiful light, and thoughtful compositions — ensuring every photograph feels genuine, timeless, and uniquely yours.
